How to Build an ATS-Friendly Resume That Gets You Hired

Tips, expert guidelines, and best practices to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and land your dream interview in 2026.

What is an ATS Resume?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by employers to scan, sort, and rank resumes before a human recruiter sees them. JobCV-SI designs clean, single-column and standardized dual-column formats that ATS parsers can read with 100% accuracy.

Target Keywords from the Job Description

Read the job post carefully and include exact keywords like technical skills (e.g. Routing & Switching, Python, BGP, CI/CD) inside your experience bullet points and skills section.

Use the XYZ Formula for Bullet Points

Instead of just writing job duties, use Google's XYZ formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". For example: "Reduced network latency by 25% by configuring OSPF cost metrics across 12 branch locations."

Single Page Rule

For freshers and professionals with under 5 years of experience, keep your resume strictly on 1 page. Recruiters spend only 6 to 7 seconds scanning your CV. JobCV-SI is optimized to fit your details cleanly on an A4 sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is JobCV-SI completely free to download resumes?

Yes, 100% free! There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or watermarks. You can build, customize, and download your CV in PDF format instantly without creating an account.

Is my personal data safe on this website?

Yes. JobCV-SI operates completely in your browser. All entered data is saved locally on your computer (using local storage). We do not store or sell your personal details to any server.

Which template is best for tech and IT roles?

The Tech & Developer Clean and Minimal ATS Plain templates work best for software engineers, network specialists, and data analysts as they prioritize clean code skills and quantifiable experience.

Should freshers include photographs on resumes?

For jobs in the US, UK, and Canada, photographs are strictly discouraged to avoid bias. However, in India, Middle East, and parts of Europe, professional headshots are accepted. You can easily toggle or omit photos on JobCV-SI.
