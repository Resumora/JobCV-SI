How to Build an ATS-Friendly Resume That Gets You Hired

Guidelines and best practices to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and land your interview.

What is an ATS Resume?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) scans, sorts, and ranks resumes before recruiters see them. Resumora uses clean single-column and standardized layouts that ATS parsers read with 100% accuracy.

Target Exact Job Keywords

Include exact technical skills mentioned in the job description (e.g. BGP, OSPF, Python Automation, CI/CD) inside your experience bullet points and skills list.

Use the XYZ Formula

Frame accomplishments using Google's XYZ formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". For example: "Reduced downtime by 35% by automating switch configuration backups."

Single Page Rule

Keep your resume strictly to 1 page if you have less than 5 years of experience. Resumora dynamically balances padding to ensure clean single-page PDF rendering.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Resumora completely free to download resumes?

Yes, 100% free! There are no hidden charges, paywalls, or watermarks. You can build and download your CV in PDF format instantly without creating an account.

Is my personal data safe on this website?

Yes. Resumora operates completely in your browser. All entered data is saved locally on your computer (via localStorage). No data is sent or stored on any external server.

Which template is best for tech and IT roles?

The Tech & Developer Clean and Minimal ATS Plain templates work best for software engineers, network specialists, and DevOps as they prioritize technical skills and clear bullet points.

Should freshers include photographs on resumes?

For US, UK, and Canadian jobs, photos are strictly discouraged to avoid bias. In India and the Middle East, professional headshots are accepted. You can easily toggle or delete photos on Resumora anytime.
